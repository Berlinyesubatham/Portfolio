My Journey

From curiosity to code.

I did not start with a plan for IT. Scroll down and walk with me, from a girl who wanted an office job to a developer building software.

Childhood · The dream

An office, long before IT

Ever since I was a kid, I wanted a job in an office. I knew nothing about IT back then. I only knew the kind of life I was aiming for.

2022 · BCA begins

The easy road, I thought

I finished 12th in 2022 and joined BCA. I believed a BCA degree would take me into IT without much struggle, and my professors said the same.

Final semester · Full-stack

First real taste of development

In my last semester I joined a full-stack course. By the end of it, I felt far more confident about building things.

After the course · Chennai

A reality check

I moved to Chennai to look for a job and quickly saw how much I still had to learn. It felt like I was starting from zero.

Back home · Starting again

Learning the basics, properly

I came back home after a month, unsure about the future. The institute had not given me the foundation I paid for, so I went back to HTML, CSS, JavaScript and C and rebuilt it on my own.

6 months after college · The opportunity

A startup says yes

About six months after finishing college, I got my chance at a startup. Since then I have worked on real projects like the INMBA platform in React, Flask and MySQL, and the SwiftPay payments app in Flutter.

Now · Dreaming bigger

Not small, and not done

My job is near my hometown, not in Chennai, and I travel two hours every morning and two every evening to do it. Comfort was never the plan. My dreams are big, and my faith keeps me steady. God is my friend and my support, and I believe He will take me high.
